## The publishers' terms, read at their own pages on 14 November 2026

**ACT Legislation Register.** Its copyright page says use of material on the Register should follow the
copyright policy of the Justice and Community Safety Directorate. That policy (justice.act.gov.au/copyright)
says content is owned by the Australian Capital Territory and licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
4.0, except the Coat of Arms, logos, third-party material and anything stated to be under other conditions.
Attribution: "© Australian Capital Territory".

**Northern Territory Legislation.** The Territory's copyright policy (legislation.nt.gov.au, Terms of Use,
in effect from 8 October 1996) grants permission to any person to publish or deal with Northern Territory
legislation on conditions, and says the Territory will not assert copyright where they are met. As the
release's licence records them: the publication does not indicate it is an official version, does not use
the arms of the Northern Territory, and reproduces the material accurately in a context that does not
mislead. The Territory may revoke, vary or withdraw the permission on reasonable notice.
